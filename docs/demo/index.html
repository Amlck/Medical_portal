EKG Systematic Interpretation
Clinical Reference
Rate Estimation

300 / (# large boxes between R waves)

300 → 150 → 100 → 75 → 60 → 50
PR Interval

Normal: 120–200 ms (3–5 small boxes)

1° AVB: >200 ms

2° Mobitz I: progressive lengthening → dropped beat

2° Mobitz II: fixed PR with dropped beats

3° AV block: complete dissociation

QRS Width

Normal: <120 ms (<3 small boxes)

RBBB: rsR′ in V1; wide S in I/V6

LBBB: broad R in I/aVL/V5–V6; no septal q

Axis (Quick)
I ↑, aVF ↑ → Normal I ↑, aVF ↓ → LAD I ↓, aVF ↑ → RAD I ↓, aVF ↓ → Extreme
QTc (Bazett)
QTc = QT ÷ √RR(s) Normal: ♂ <440ms, ♀ <460ms Prolonged: >500ms = high risk
LVH Voltage

SV1 + RV5 or RV6 >35 mm

R in aVL >11 mm

R in I + S in III >25 mm

Sgarbossa (LBBB)

≥5 pts = likely ischemia

+5: concordant STE ≥1mm

+3: concordant STD ≥1mm in V1–V3

+2: discordant STE ≥5mm

Wellens Syndrome

Type A: biphasic T in V2–V3

Type B: deep symmetric T inversion V2–V3

→ Critical LAD stenosis; avoid stress testing
